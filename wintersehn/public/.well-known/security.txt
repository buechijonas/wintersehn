# Security policy for wintersehn.ch (RFC 9116)
#
# We welcome responsible security research (ethical hacking) on
# wintersehn.ch. We will not pursue legal action against anyone who searches
# for vulnerabilities in good faith and follows the rules below.
#
# Rules:
#  - Report findings promptly to security@wintersehn.ch and give us
#    reasonable time (90 days) to fix them before any public disclosure.
#  - Only access, modify or delete data as far as needed to demonstrate the
#    issue; never touch other users' data and delete anything you obtained.
#  - No denial of service, spam, social engineering or physical attacks.
#  - Use your own test accounts; do not degrade the service for others.
#  - Include steps to reproduce, affected URLs and the potential impact.
